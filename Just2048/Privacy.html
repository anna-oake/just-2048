Privacy policy

Developer: Anna Oake

The app does not send personal data to the developer and has no tracking or analytics.

Local data

Your board, scores, game state and cached Game Center player identifiers are stored on your device to save progress, separate players’ scores and retry score submissions. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Game Center

When signed in, the app sends your best score to Apple’s Game Center and retrieves your account’s best score. Apple associates scores with your account and displays leaderboard rankings under Game Center & Privacy. Manage Game Center data and privacy through your Apple account and Game Center settings.

Contact and deletion

Support emails are used to answer your request and retained only as needed to resolve it or meet legal obligations. Email anna@oa.ke to request deletion of correspondence or developer-held beta feedback.
